Quick clinician tips

Start with a preferred item or action, wait 5–10 seconds, and model a response without requiring speech. Acknowledge any intentional communication. Use one-choice teaching before testing with two choices if needed. The playful animation is an engagement opportunity—not evidence of joint attention by itself.

The pictured emoji are simple visual symbols and may not be equally recognizable to every student. Real photos or familiar objects may be better for some learners.
