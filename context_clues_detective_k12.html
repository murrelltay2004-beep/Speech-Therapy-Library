Clinician guide: six context clue types

Definition: the word is directly explained. Synonym: nearby words have similar meaning. Antonym: an opposite or contrast helps. Example: specific examples illustrate the meaning. Inference: details support a logical guess. Cause and effect: a reason or result helps explain the word.
