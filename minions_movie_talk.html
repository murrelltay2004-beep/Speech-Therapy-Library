🍌 Minions Movie Talk

Interactive speech & language activity inspired by the 2015 animated movie Minions

Designed for students familiar with the 2015 movie. Read prompts aloud if needed. Clinician can accept reasonable alternative responses; the sample answers are guides.

🎬 That's a wrap!

Clinician notes & scoring

In multiple-choice mode, the first selected response is scored automatically. Open-ended mode allows the clinician to mark Independent, With cues, or Not yet. The score reflects first attempts and is not a standardized measure. The movie details are simplified for therapy; students can receive credit for other well-supported answers.

Questions include literal comprehension, making inferences, explaining motives, sequencing events, cause/effect, practical solutions, conversational questions, and vocabulary. New rounds shuffle the prompts.
