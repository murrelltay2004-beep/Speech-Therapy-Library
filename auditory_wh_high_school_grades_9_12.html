🎧 Listen & Solve!

Grades 9–12 • Age-respectful stories • Simple WH questions

For grades 9–12: age-appropriate topics with concrete Who, What, Where, and When questions. Hide the story and read it aloud or use Listen. No student reading required.

Listen carefully, then answer the questions.

Clinician notes and suggested use

Play the story once, then ask one WH question at a time. Repeat as needed. For an easier task, show two verbal choices. For expressive practice, switch to open response. Correct answers and response support are shown after scoring. Progress counts responses during the current story.

Questions target explicit details rather than inference. Each level includes six high-school-relevant stories. Questions target explicitly stated information, not complex inference. New Story changes the passage when possible.
