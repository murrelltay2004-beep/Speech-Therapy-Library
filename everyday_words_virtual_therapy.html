Simple, functional vocabulary for virtual speech therapy. Show one task at a time. The clinician controls scoring; students can respond by pointing, speaking, signing, or AAC.

Session summary

Independent and supported responses are separated. “No response” means an opportunity was presented; “not attempted” means it was not meaningfully presented. Track joint attention separately from simply looking at the screen.

Ideas for use: Start with five opportunities. Model responses without requiring speech. For requesting, pause and offer an immediately available choice or action; don't withhold essential items. For shared attention, take turns selecting pictures or making the reward appear and observe whether the student coordinates attention with you.
